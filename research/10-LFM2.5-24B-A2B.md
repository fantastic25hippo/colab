# 10 — LFM2.5-24B-A2B: Checkpoints, L4 Speeds and TPU Feasibility

Consolidated from `LFM2.5-24B-A2B GPU Checkpoint Comparison.pdf` (7 pages) and `LFM2-5-24B-A2B-Summary.md`. The model is LiquidAI **LFM2.5-24B-A2B**: a hybrid short-convolution + GQA MoE with 24.0B total parameters, ~2.45B active per token, 64 experts with 4 active per layer, 38 MoE layers, 30 short-conv layers, 10 attention layers and 2 dense FFNs. Checkpoints audited: `urakozz/LFM2.5-24B-A2B-W4A16-g64`, `sapidLabs/LFM2.5-24B-A2B-NVFP4-W4A16`, `GestaltLabs/LFM2.5-24B-A2B-NVFP4-W4A16`. Numbers are **Reported** estimates from one Gemini analysis, not local runs.

## 1. Why the model suits an L4

- **L4:** AD104, sm_89, 22.5 GB usable GDDR6 (24 GB physical with ECC), 300 GB/s, 48 MB L2, 121 TFLOPS BF16/FP16, 242.5 TFLOPS FP8.
- **Active-bandwidth amplification:** a dense 24B model would stream ~20–22 GB/token and cap near 15–20 tok/s. This MoE streams only ~2.45B active params (~1.3–1.6 GB), so the theoretical ceiling is >150 tok/s and realistic pre-optimization speed is 60–90+ tok/s.
- **L2 synergy:** ~719M shared parameters (attention projections, short-conv kernels, routers) are active every token. Marlin tile ordering keeps L2 hit rate at 80–95% while packed 4-bit expert weights stream via `cp.async` into SMEM.

## 2. Audit of the three checkpoints

**A. NVFP4 checkpoints also use Marlin on L4.** The common misconception is that NVFP4 needs Blackwell sm_100/120 FP4 tensor cores. Both NVFP4 repos are **weight-only W4A16**: 4-bit E2M1 weights + one FP8 E4M3 micro-scale per 16 weights + one FP32 tensor scale = **4.50 bits/weight**, with BF16 activations. Ada sm_89 runs them through Marlin dequantization; both repos force each expert's gate (`w1`) and up (`w3`) to share a per-tensor scale so vLLM/SGLang fuse `w1/w3` and dispatch Marlin W4A16. Precision: urakozz is symmetric INT4 g64 at 4.25 bpw; NVFP4 is non-uniform E2M1 with block 16 (4× finer than g64) for only +0.25 bits.

**B. VRAM headroom — why keeping attention in BF16 matters.** urakozz is 13.3 GB vs 14.7–14.95 GB for the NVFP4 repos. The saving comes from quantizing **all 7,402 linears**, including the 10 attention and 30 short-conv layers; sapid/Gestalt leave those in BF16 and quantize only FFN/MoE (~96% of params). The hybrid KV math makes that trade unnecessary: 30 short-conv layers use fixed recurrent state independent of context; only 10 GQA layers (8 KV heads) scale with context. BF16 KV is **~40 KB/token ≈ 5.1 GB at 131K**; FP8 KV is **~20 KB/token ≈ 2.6 GB at 131K**. Even 14.7–15.9 GB of weights leaves 6.5–8.0 GB free on L4 — enough for full 131K plus CUDA graphs. Trading BF16 on 40 layers to save 1.4 GB you do not need is a bad accuracy trade.

**C. Maturity.** urakozz unfuses every expert projection into 7,402 separate linears so AutoRound can run; its card says “vLLM: not yet tested” and “accuracy against bf16 has not been measured”, and calibration is OpenCodeInstruct (code-only). GestaltLabs ran an MSE FP8-scale sweep plus 512 `ultrachat_200k` samples, claims **98.6% BF16 accuracy** across four benchmarks, and was verified on SGLang Marlin. sapidLabs packed 38 routed MoE + 2 dense FFN into ModelOpt `modelopt_mixed`, reports **13.75 GiB resident in vLLM**, and was explicitly tested on vLLM Marlin W4A16.

## 4. Which checkpoint for which engine

- **vLLM → sapidLabs.** 13.75 GiB resident is nearly urakozz-sized, all attention/conv stay BF16, and vLLM maps `modelopt_mixed` to fused Marlin W4A16 without the unfused workaround.
- **SGLang → GestaltLabs.** Most rigorous calibration, calibrated FP8 KV scales, 98.6% BF16 recovery, validated on SGLang Marlin.
- **Standalone HF Transformers → urakozz.** Loads with `AutoModelForCausalLM.from_pretrained()` in transformers ≥5.x plus auto-round/gptqmodel Marlin, without ModelOpt FP8 tensor scales.

## 5. Speed on L4

Raw ranking is **urakozz > sapidLabs > GestaltLabs**. The NVFP4 repos keep non-expert layers in BF16 for accuracy, and on a 300 GB/s bandwidth-bound GPU that costs more than NVFP4's finer scales save.

Per-token bytes at B=1, from `config.json` (2.455B active of 23.97B total; 38 MoE blocks read 4/64 experts, but every non-MoE layer is 100% dense):

| Component | urakozz | sapidLabs | GestaltLabs |
| :--- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Tied `lm_head` + routers/norms (267.12M, BF16) | 0.534 GB | 0.534 GB | 0.534 GB |
| 30 short-conv + 10 GQA (688.17M) | 0.323 GB | 1.216 GB | 1.216 GB |
| 2 dense SwiGLU L0/L1 (144.70M) | 0.077 GB | 0.081 GB | 0.289 GB |
| 38 routed MoE, 4/64 active (1,434.45M) | 0.762 GB | 0.807 GB | 0.807 GB |
| **Active weights/token** | **1.696 GB** | **2.639 GB** | **2.847 GB** |

FP8 KV adds **10.24 KB/context token** (41.9 MB at 4K, 335.5 MB at 32K). Marlin reaches ~80–85% of GDDR6 bus utilization on linear layers; after MoE sorting, short-conv updates and CUDA-graph overhead, the estimate is ~75–82% for INT4 Marlin and ~72–78% for emulated NVFP4.

**Speculative decoding is strictly beneficial at B=1** because the L4's 121 BF16 TFLOPS sits >98% idle during memory-bound decode. N-Gram lookup costs 0 MB and matches 2–5-token spans from the prompt, codebase or tool schemas. LiquidAI's official **DSpark 328M** sidecar (664 MB, block size 9, `--speculative-draft-model-path LiquidAI/LFM2.5-24B-A2B-DSpark --speculative-dspark-block-size 9 --speculative-num-draft-tokens 10`) also accelerates free-form reasoning and fits in the 6.5–9.0 GB free VRAM.

## 6. Running 200K Context on 1× TPU v5e-1 (15.75 GiB): Hybrid W8A8 + W4A16 + K8V4 Pipeline

A code-level audit across `vllm-project/tpu-inference` (`v0.32.0`), `vllm` (`lfm2_moe.py`, `short_conv.py`), and `llama.cpp` (`ggml-quants.c`) confirms that `LFM2.5-24B-A2B` can run at **200,000-token context** (`B=1`) on a single TPU v5e-1 (`15.748 GiB` HBM, `32 MB` VMEM) by combining **four targeted interventions**:

### A. Exact Subsystem Memory & Bandwidth Budget at 200K Context (`B=1`)
In `LFM2.5-24B-A2B` (`23.93B` weight params), the **38 routed MoE expert blocks** (`64 experts × 3 × 2048 × 1536`) hold **22.95B params (95.74%)**, while the **40 shared backbone blocks** (`30 ShortConv` = `503.5M`, `10 GQA` = `62.9M`, `2 Dense SwiGLU FFNs` in L0–L1 = `144.7M`) hold only **0.711B params (2.97%)** and execute on every token. Keeping the 40 shared backbone layers in **W8A8 Dynamic INT8** (running on the native **393 TOPS INT8 MXU**) and the 38 MoE layers in **W4A16** adds only `+0.31 GiB` HBM while preserving 8-bit accuracy on sensitive conv/attn layers:

| Subsystem (`Lfm2MoeForCausalLM`) | Precision / Scheme | Static HBM (GiB) | Stream / Token @ 200K | Hardware Unit |
| :--- | :--- | ---: | ---: | :--- |
| **38 Routed MoE Layers** (`22.95B` tot / `1.43B` act) | `W4A16` (`g128` re-blocked or `g32` exact QAD-Q4_0) | **10.69 GiB** (`12.02` g32) | 0.717 GB (`0.807` g32) | VPU unpack in VMEM → BF16 MXU |
| **30 ShortConv + 10 GQA + 2 Dense FFN** (`0.711B`) | `W8A8` per-channel INT8 + dynamic token INT8 | **0.66 GiB** | 0.712 GB | **Native 393 TOPS INT8 MXU** |
| **Tied `embed_tokens` / `lm_head`** (`128000 × 2048`) | `BF16` (`0.49 GiB`) or `INT4 g32` (`0.14 GiB`) | **0.49 GiB** (`0.14` emb4) | 0.524 GB (`0.148` emb4) | HBM gather / BF16 MXU |
| **MoE Routers (`gate`), `expert_bias`, Norms + Conv State** | `BF16` (`conv_state` `[30, 2048, 2]` = `0.24 MiB`) | **0.01 GiB** | 0.011 GB | VPU |
| **200K GQA KV Cache** (`10 layers, 8 KV heads, D=64`) | **`K8V4`** (`INT8` K + `INT4` V + `BF16` `g64` scales) | **1.49 GiB** (`1.91` RPA-aligned) | **1.600 GB** (vs `4.10 GB` BF16) | **INT8 MXU ($QK^T$) + VMEM unpack ($PV$)** |
| **Total (`Weights + 200K KV`, leaving `1.9–2.4 GiB` XLA)** | **Hybrid `W8A8` + `W4A16` + `K8V4`** | **13.34 GiB (`g128`) / 14.32 GiB (`g32+emb4`)** | **3.56 GB/tok (~155–215 tok/s)** | Fits inside `14.49 GiB` (`0.92` util) ceiling |

### B. The Four Required Code & Weight Interventions
1. **Lossless Streaming Repack of `LFM2.5-24B-A2B-QAD-Q4_0.gguf` (13.58 GB):**
   - **Split-half nibble un-permutation:** `GGML_TYPE_Q4_0` (`18 B` per 32 weights = `2 B` FP16 scale `d` + `16 B` `qs`) stores weights `0..15` in the low nibbles (`qs & 0x0F`) and weights `16..31` in the high nibbles (`(qs >> 4) & 0x0F`). Concatenate `np.concatenate([lo, hi], axis=-1)` before packing 8 unsigned nibbles into `compressed-tensors` `int32` (`packed |= (u & 0xF) << (4*k)`).
   - **3D Expert Slicing & Naming:** Slice GGUF `blk.{2..39}.ffn_{gate,down,up}_exps.weight` (`[64, out, in]`) into `vllm`'s required `model.layers.{i}.feed_forward.experts.{0..63}.{w1,w2,w3}.{weight_packed,weight_scale,weight_shape}`, and map `attn_output` $\rightarrow$ `self_attn.out_proj` and `output_norm` $\rightarrow$ `model.embedding_norm`.
2. **Multi-Group `compressed-tensors` Config Collision Bypass (vLLM Issue #28197):**
   - `CompressedTensorsWNA16MoEMethod` inspects `target_scheme_map["Linear"]`. Place `"Linear"` and `"re:.*feed_forward\\.experts.*"` in `group_1_w4a16_moe_experts`, and use explicit prefix regexes (`self_attn`, `conv`, and layers `0..1` `feed_forward`) in `group_0_w8a8_int8_backbone` so regex matching routes all 40 backbone layers to `VllmCompressedTensorsW8A8Int8LinearMethod` while MoE experts get `WNA16`.
3. **In-VMEM `K8V4` / `INT8` Pallas Attention & Issue #3126 Fix (`kv_cache_manager.py` + `rpa_v3`):**
   - Dequantizing `k_int8.to(bf16)` outside the kernel at 200K spills **410 MB/layer** to HBM (>12× the 32 MB VMEM).
   - Patch `kv_cache_manager.py` to honor `int8` on v5e and use the Pallas `K8V4` flash-decode / RPA v3 inner loop (`B_k=512` tiles = `<300 KB` in VMEM): dynamically quantize $Q$ per head to `int8`, execute $Q_{\text{int8}} K_{\text{int8}}^T$ on the **393 TOPS INT8 MXU** (`preferred_element_type=jnp.int32`, 16× fewer VPU ops), absorb the per-token-head $V$ scale into softmax probabilities (`p_scaled = p * v_scale`), and unpack 4-bit $V$ nibbles strictly in VMEM.
4. **Pure-XLA `ShortConv.forward` Patch for `MODEL_IMPL_TYPE=vllm` (`torchax`):**
   - Upstream `vllm/model_executor/layers/mamba/short_conv.py` dispatches Triton (`causal_conv1d_fn`) or a Python loop slicing by traced `query_start_loc[i]` (`ConcretizationTypeError`), and XLA `PAD_SLOT_ID = -1` in `state_indices_tensor` wraps around to corrupt `conv_state[-1]`. Replace `ShortConv.forward` with a branch-free XLA depthwise 3-tap conv (`bx = B_gate * x_in`) using `safe_idx = torch.clamp(state_indices, min=0)` and masked state updates, and launch with `--no-enable-prefix-caching`.

## 8. Caveats

- All throughput figures are **estimates** from a Gemini analysis; none were measured in this repo. (**In-repo L4 status (`../benchmarks/LFM2.5-24B-A2B-Q4/L4/RESULTS.md`):** 16 shards of `LFM2.5-24B-A2B-UD-Q4_K_XL.gguf` (14.88 GB) downloaded into `/dev/shm` in **16.60 s (896 MB/s)**, but `llama-server` CUDA 12.8 timed out at 180 s due to a CUDA 13.0 driver mismatch; decode speed is not yet measured.)
- CU rates disagree with `01`: this document uses L4 ~1.71 and v5e-1 ~1.20–1.75 CU/h; the measured tier table says L4 1.54 and v5e-1 2.92 CU/h. See `11`.
- The source mixes GiB and GB (“16.0 GiB / 17.18 GB” vs “16 GB HBM2”). Use 15.75 GiB usable for planning.
- “98.6% BF16 accuracy” is the GestaltLabs model-card claim, not an independent eval. “0.095 mean relative error” is sapidLabs' own RTN metric.
- `NIM_ALLOW_NVFP4_EMULATION=1` is required for Marlin emulation on Ada; without it vLLM may reject the checkpoint. DSpark is SGLang-only in this write-up; the vLLM path uses N-Gram.
